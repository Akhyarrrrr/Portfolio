# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers, engineering leads, and prospective clients evaluating Akhyar for remote software-engineering work.

## Product Purpose

Present a truthful, recruiter-friendly view of Akhyar's experience, projects, technical range, and availability. Success means visitors can quickly understand his positioning, inspect credible project evidence, and contact him without inflated claims or broken demonstrations.

## Brand Personality

Practical, capable, and honest.

## Anti-references

Generic framework-first developer portfolios, inflated impact claims, decorative AI-generated layouts, and project galleries that hide older work without explanation.

## Design Principles

- Lead with problems solved, not framework names.
- Show evidence and limitations clearly.
- Make flagship work obvious without hiding the broader body of work.
- Keep recruiter paths fast, readable, and predictable.
- Use motion and technical visuals only when they improve understanding.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast, complete keyboard navigation, visible focus states, semantic structure, responsive layouts, and meaningful reduced-motion behavior.
